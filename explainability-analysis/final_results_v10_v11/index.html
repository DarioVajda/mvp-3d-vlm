Geometry fusion v10/v11: final results

Four ways of fusing VGGT geometry into Qwen3.5-2B, each trained with two seeds and scored on the full VSI-Bench (5,130 questions, 32 frames) at three checkpoints. At the final checkpoint the four arms are tied, and cross-attention's early lead on relative direction is gone.

Checkpoints at steps 5,750, 8,250 and 10,750, each after a 750-step learning-rate decay. Results as of 8 Nov 2026.
Bottom line. Overall, the arms span 0.5 points (70.3–70.8), less than the gap between two seeds of the same arm (up to 1.4). Additive is nominally first. Cross-attention led relative direction at step 5,750 on both seeds. By step 10,750 additive leads that task too. An inference-time ablation at step 5,750 found the same: the learned cross-cell attention isn't needed, but each cell's own geometry is.

Relative direction: the lead that closed

Relative direction was the one task where cross-attention led on both seeds. Over the last 2,500 steps additive gained about 2.4 points there, and every cross-attention variant fell or stayed flat.

Why the attention doesn't help: the swap ablation

At step 5,750 (seed 0, before the lead closed), we scored the xa3_qknu arm again with its learned cross-attention replaced at inference, keeping all its weights. Relative direction: 968 questions. Bars show accuracy, whiskers the bootstrap 95% CI of the change against the learned model.

Relative direction with the learned attention swapped out
xa3_qknu seed 0, step 5,750, 968 questions; dashed line: additive seed 0 at the same step (85.4)
  • Removing the learned query·key term (bias only) or all reading from other cells (own cell only) leaves accuracy within noise. The questions lost and gained nearly cancel (26/20 and 28/25).
  • Replacing each cell's geometry with the scene mean costs 24 points. The model depends on per-cell geometry, which is exactly what additive injection provides. Part of this drop may come from feeding the model an out-of-distribution input, and a no-geometry control would separate the two.
  • The two longer-training results agree: once additive is trained longer it matches cross-attention, because the per-cell pathway is what both arms rely on.

Caveats

  • Two seeds per arm. Differences between arms are about the size of the seed spread, so the ranking is not significant.
  • The swap ablation was run on one arm, one seed and one checkpoint (step 5,750), and only at inference.
  • The xa3_nu step-10,750 evals come from a separate re-run (job 217416). The automatic eval was killed because slurm/decay_eval.sh was edited while the branch was running. Training had finished and both eval files are complete.
  • Overall = the benchmark's own summary (mean over task types), so it isn't the per-question mean.
