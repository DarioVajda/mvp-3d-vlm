Two Direction Questions, Up Close

Two "hard" relative-direction questions from VSI-Bench, chosen because the two models disagree and every named object is visible: in one only cross-attention (xa3_qknu) is right, in the other only the additive model is. Each is followed through SAM 3 object masks, saliency, cross-attention and J-lens readouts at ten decoder layers.

What the two questions show

  • Both models answer in letter space, late. At the answer position the option letters only take over from layer 17. Before that the J-lens reads generic words ("Option", "Neutral"). Neither model reads a direction word (front, back, left, right) as its top choice there; they mostly sit in the hundreds or thousands.
  • The cross-attention failure is decided by layer 17. In question 3655 it reads "B" (back-left) at layer 17 already, and "back" climbs to rank 46 at its answer position, against rank 879 in the additive model, which answers C correctly with 98% confidence.
  • In the success, the target's cells read from the standing point. In question 3584 ("standing by the window"), at layer 0 the towel's cells put 10× more attention on the window than the 3D bias alone would give, and the backpack's cells 5.7× more. In the failure the target's cells read the standing point (the printer) only at bias level. Two questions with noisy masks make this a lead, not a result.
  • Salient cells read as the question's objects; the geometry doesn't read as words. At the five most salient cells the question's object words rank 0–10 at most layers (up to about 140 late in 3584), against hundreds to tens of thousands at random cells. In 3584 the most salient cell reads "towel" at every layer from 0 to 22. The geometry each model adds is 13–57% of a cell's activation, yet reads as noise tokens.

Cross-attention between the objects

J-lens across layers

The J-lens (Anthropic, "A global workspace in language models") turns a hidden state at layer ℓ into a ranking of all 151k tokens: rank(J_ℓ h), where J_ℓ is the average Jacobian from layer ℓ to the last layer, fitted on this checkpoint. Rank 0 means the token is the lens's top reading. Both lenses are fitted as in v7 (text: 30 Wikipedia passages; visual: 3 VSI videos), so they are coarse.

Interpretation

How far this goes

  • Two questions, one seed. Everything here is an example. Run-to-run noise in the maps is 6–12% (bf16 on different GPUs), and the cross-attention "success" in 3584 is narrow (P = 0.48).
  • Masks are noisy, in 3655 especially: the printer mask spills onto the desk and notice boards, the door onto an air-conditioning unit and shelves. Object-level numbers inherit these errors.
  • Small masks inflate enrichment. The towel in 3584 covers only 3.4 cells per frame, so a few salient cells on it give 9–15×.
  • Coarse lenses. 30 text passages and 3 videos per lens, as in v7; ranks in the hundreds are weak evidence either way.
  • Next: the same measures over all 179 object questions with SAM 3, comparing "target reads from start" between correct and wrong answers.
